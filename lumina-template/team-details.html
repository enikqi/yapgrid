Design Director

Ava Lindqvist

  1. Home
  2. Team
  3. Ava Lindqvist

Ava founded Lumina in 2016 after eight years leading design inside product teams. She works mainly on brand systems and the interfaces that have to carry them.

Her background is unusual for a design director: she started in front-end engineering and moved sideways. It shows in how she works — she prototypes in the browser, argues about component boundaries, and has never once handed an engineer a design that could not be built.

Outside the studio she teaches an interface design module at a local university and maintains two small open-source tools that nobody asked for but several thousand people use.

Focus areas

01

Design systems

Token architecture, component APIs and the governance that keeps a system alive past its first year.

02

Complex interfaces

Dashboards, admin tools and anything where the user is an expert under time pressure.

Making an identity survive contact with a real interface, rather than living only on a pitch deck.
